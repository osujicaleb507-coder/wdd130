Nigeria

Nigeria is Africa's most populous nation and a major economic and cultural hub located in West Africa. Centered around its capital, Abuja, and its largest financial metropolis, Lagos, it features a diverse population of over 220 million people, an economy powered by petroleum, technology, and finance, and massive global cultural influence driven by Afrobeats music and Nollywood cinema.
